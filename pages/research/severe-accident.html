Severe Accident

MELCOR·MAAP·CINEMA 기반 사고 해석, 사고관리 전략 검토와 해양 MSR 안전 연구

ATHENA investigates severe accident progression, containment behavior and fission product transport using integrated analysis codes and numerical models. The September 2026 program emphasizes code verification, uncertainty analysis and the evaluation of accident management measures. Molten salt work extends to marine reactor concepts and fuel salt spill behavior.

Integrated severe accident analysis

원전 입력모델 개발과 사고 진행·격납건물 응답 해석

  • MELCOR
  • MAAP
  • CINEMA

We develop plant input models and simulate severe accident sequences using MELCOR, MAAP and the Korean integrated code CINEMA. The analyses connect reactor-system thermal hydraulics with core degradation, containment response and fission product behavior.

The program includes comparison of predicted phenomena and assessment of mitigation strategies. These calculations also provide data for AI-based accident management support.

CINEMA verification and uncertainty analysis

국산 중대사고 해석코드 CINEMA 검증과 불확실도·핵분열생성물 거동 분석

  • CINEMA
  • Verification
  • Fission products

We examine the predicted behavior of the CINEMA integrated severe accident code through verification and uncertainty studies. The research considers system responses, severe accident phenomena and the behavior of fission products.

CINEMA links reactor-system, containment and fission product calculations. The analysis therefore also examines how model assumptions and coupled responses affect the interpretation of accident sequences and mitigation options.

Hydrogen risk and accident management

수소 위험도와 격납건물 현상을 분석하고 사고관리 조치의 효과를 평가

  • Hydrogen risk
  • Containment
  • SAMG

Containment research includes hydrogen risk assessment and numerical analysis of severe accident phenomena. We investigate how accident conditions and mitigation actions affect containment integrity and fission product behavior.

We also develop support tools for Severe Accident Management Guidance (SAMG). Strategy studies examine the feasibility and effectiveness of mitigation measures under the modeled conditions.

Molten salt spill, spreading and solidification

용융염 누출 시 퍼짐·고화·열전달과 핵분열생성물 이동 현상 해석

  • Molten salt spill
  • Fluent
  • MPS

For molten salt reactor incidents, we investigate the spreading and solidification of leaked salt and its heat transfer to the receiving structure. The numerical work compares an Eulerian approach using ANSYS Fluent with a Lagrangian approach using the Moving Particle Semi-Implicit (MPS) method.

The work links the salt flow and thermal response with the broader containment phenomena of splashing, vaporization, aerosol generation and deposition. The illustrations show specific modeled incident conditions rather than a universal prediction for all MSR designs.

Safety research for the maritime MARS-100 concept

100 MWth MARS-100 개념과 연계한 해양 MSR 안전계통·누출 사고 연구

  • Marine MSR
  • MARS-100
  • Safety systems

Marine MSR safety-system research includes the 100 MWth maritime molten salt reactor concept MARS-100. Salt spill analysis and the assessment of cooling and solidification behavior form part of this research direction.

Related ocean-motion studies examine PMFR core behavior and freeze-valve seal loss and drainage. These topics connect marine operating conditions with the safety functions of molten salt systems.
