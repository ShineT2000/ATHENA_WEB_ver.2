Reactor Design

ATHENA combines thermal-hydraulic experiments with CFD, system-code simulations, and coupled reactor physics analysis to develop advanced reactor concepts. We use experimental data and numerical predictions to evaluate flow and heat removal and guide component design and system optimization.

PMFR Design and Thermal-Hydraulic Optimization

PMFR의 자연순환 실험·노물리-열수력 연계 해석·열교환기 및 1차 계통 최적화를 연결하는 설계 연구

  • PMFR
  • Natural circulation
  • Design optimization

Within the i-SAFE-MSR program, ATHENA investigates the thermal-hydraulic and safety design of the Passive Molten Salt Fast Reactor (PMFR). Fuel salt heats in the lower core, rises through the central riser and returns through the outer downcomer, transferring heat to the helical-coil heat exchanger. The design studies examine how the reactor layout and component geometry affect circulation and heat removal.

Natural circulation experiments and scaling

We use adiabatic two-phase loops and bubble visualization to examine how helium injection changes circulation. Laboratory and larger-loop configurations using molten salt fuel simulants support scaling studies and provide thermal-hydraulic data for model assessment.

Coupled reactor physics and thermal-hydraulic analysis

Our coupled workflow links OpenFOAM thermal-hydraulic calculations with reactor physics results from Serpent and OpenMC. It evaluates the interaction between power distribution, fuel salt temperature and circulation, providing a basis for comparing core and flow-path designs.

Heat exchanger and primary-loop optimization

Helical-coil heat exchanger studies examine the balance between heat transfer and flow resistance in the primary loop. Pareto design studies compare candidate designs to identify thermal-hydraulic tradeoffs.

These design choices are considered alongside PCM-based passive safety to connect normal-operation performance with decay heat removal requirements.

Bubble-Assisted Fission Product Removal

기포 부상에 의한 불용성 핵분열생성물 제거 실험과 유체·기포·입자의 3상 거동 해석

  • Fission products
  • Bubble-particle interaction
  • MPS

We investigate the flotation and capture of insoluble fission products using helium bubbles. Experiments with particle simulants examine changes in concentration and the influence of particle size, helium flow rate and initial concentration.

The Moving Particle Semi-Implicit (MPS) model addresses fluid, bubbles and particles, including particle attachment to bubble surfaces. Experimental observations support verification and validation of the in-house model used to study removal behavior.

Natural-Circulation SMR and Flooding Safety System

자연순환 SMR의 침수안전계통 구성과 실험시설 개념 설계

  • Natural circulation
  • Flooding safety system
  • Conceptual design

ATHENA studies the system configuration of a natural-circulation SMR with a metal containment vessel and an on-demand flooding safety system (FSS). The design work examines the heat removal path provided by flooding around the containment and its integration with the reactor system.

System-code models inform design choices and the conceptual design of a dedicated experimental facility, with experiments planned as a subsequent step. Accident-condition performance and containment heat transfer validation are addressed in Safety Analysis.

This program builds on earlier SMR studies of passive residual heat removal, two-phase air cooling for a supercritical CO2 cycle and pulsating heat pipes.

Marine Motion Effects and Freeze-Valve Performance

해양 운동 조건에 따른 노심 응답과 동결밸브의 밀봉·염 배출 성능 평가

  • Marine MSR
  • Coupled analysis
  • Freeze valve

Using the coupled PMFR model, we assess how rolling motion changes core power distribution, fuel salt temperature and reactivity.

Freeze-valve studies evaluate seal loss and salt drainage under roll, pitch and translational motions. These studies address drainage performance for marine applications. Related marine MSR accident research examines salt spill, spreading and solidification.
