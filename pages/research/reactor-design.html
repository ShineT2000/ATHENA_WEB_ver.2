Reactor Design

열수력 실험과 CFD·계통코드·노물리 연계 해석으로 유동·열제거 성능을 평가하고 원자로 설계를 개선합니다.

ATHENA combines thermal-hydraulic experiments with CFD, system-code simulations, and coupled reactor physics analysis to develop advanced reactor concepts. We use experimental data and numerical predictions to evaluate flow and heat removal and guide component design and system optimization.

Passive Molten Salt Fast Reactor (PMFR)

i-SAFE-MSR 연구의 세 축: 자연순환 성능, 불용성 핵분열생성물 거동, 피동안전·열성능 최적화

  • PMFR
  • i-SAFE-MSR
  • Natural circulation

Within the i-SAFE-MSR research program, ATHENA investigates the thermal-hydraulic and safety design of the PMFR. Fuel salt heats in the lower core, rises through the central riser and transfers heat at the helical-coil heat exchanger before returning through the downcomer. Helium bubbling is investigated both to enhance circulation and to assist fission product removal.

The work connects component experiments with reactor physics and thermal-hydraulic calculations. Accident prevention and passive decay heat removal are design objectives evaluated through these studies.

Helium bubbling and natural circulation

비가열 2상 자연순환 실험과 용융염 모사 유체 루프를 이용한 헬륨 버블링 효과·상사법칙 평가

  • Two-phase experiment
  • OpenFOAM
  • Scaling laws

We study how injected helium changes two-phase flow and the circulation of the working fluid. The program combines adiabatic natural-circulation experiments, visual observation of bubbles and OpenFOAM simulations of velocity and gas volume fraction.

Laboratory and larger loop configurations support scaling studies for molten salt fuel simulants. These comparisons help identify thermal-hydraulic parameters needed when moving from an experimental loop to a reactor concept.

Coupled reactor physics and thermal-hydraulic analysis

OpenFOAM과 Serpent·OpenMC를 연계해 출력 분포와 열수력 피드백을 평가

  • OpenFOAM
  • Serpent
  • OpenMC

Liquid fuel links the heat source directly to the flow field. Our analysis couples reactor physics calculations with thermal-hydraulic models to evaluate the distribution of heat generation, fuel salt circulation and heat transfer.

The coupled workflow combines OpenFOAM with Serpent and OpenMC. The coupled approach also provides a basis for investigating changes in power distribution and temperature under marine motion.

Insoluble fission products and bubble-assisted removal

기포 부상에 의한 불용성 핵분열생성물 제거 실험과 유체·기포·나노입자의 3상 거동 해석

  • Fission products
  • Helium bubbling
  • MPS

We investigate the flotation and capture of insoluble fission products using helium bubbles. Experiments examine particle concentration and the influence of particle size, helium flow rate and initial concentration.

Numerical development uses the Moving Particle Semi-Implicit (MPS) method to represent fluid, bubbles and particles, including particle attachment to bubble surfaces. Verification and validation of the in-house model accompany the experimental studies.

PMFR safety and thermal performance optimization

나선형 열교환기·1차 계통의 열수력 성능과 PCM 피동안전계통을 함께 고려하는 설계 최적화

  • Helical-coil heat exchanger
  • Design tradeoffs
  • Passive safety

The PMFR design studies consider heat exchanger geometry and primary-loop thermal hydraulics together. Pareto design studies compare competing thermal and flow requirements rather than selecting geometry from heat transfer alone.

This work connects with PCM-based passive safety studies and the investigation of fission product removal. Together, these topics address the relationship between normal-operation thermal performance and decay heat removal requirements.

Natural-circulation SMRs and flooding safety concepts

자연순환 SMR의 침수안전계통 개념 설계와 실험시설 기획

  • Natural circulation
  • Flooding safety system
  • Conceptual design

ATHENA investigates a natural-circulation SMR with a metal containment vessel and a flooding safety system (FSS). The concept uses flooding around the containment to provide an additional heat removal path during an accident. Reactor and FSS behavior are evaluated with system-code models.

A dedicated experimental facility is in conceptual design, with experiments planned as a subsequent step. Earlier SMR research also examined passive residual heat removal, two-phase air cooling for a supercritical CO2 cycle and pulsating heat pipes.

Ocean motion effects on PMFR and freeze valves

해양 운동에 따른 노심 출력·온도 변화와 동결밸브의 밀봉 상실·배출 거동 평가

  • Marine MSR
  • Coupled analysis
  • Freeze valve

The PMFR program extends to the effects of ocean motion. Coupled analyses examine changes in the core power distribution, temperature and reactivity under rolling motion.

Separate freeze-valve studies compare seal loss and drainage under different motions, including roll, pitch and translational motion. These studies inform the assessment of fuel salt drainage for marine applications. Related marine MSR accident research examines salt spill and solidification.
