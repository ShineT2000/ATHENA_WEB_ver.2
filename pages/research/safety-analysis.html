Safety Analysis

열수력 실험과 CFD·계통코드 해석으로 사고 시 원자로와 피동안전계통의 냉각 성능을 평가하고, 실험 검증과 불확실도 분석으로 안전해석의 신뢰도를 높입니다.

ATHENA combines thermal-hydraulic experiments with CFD and system-code simulations to assess reactor cooling and passive safety under accident conditions. We validate analytical models against experimental data and quantify uncertainties to evaluate safety-system performance.

Safety assessment of the innovative SMR

i-SMR 설계기준사고 해석과 피동안전계통 성능 평가

  • i-SMR
  • Design basis accident
  • Passive safety

We develop plant and safety-system models to investigate the response of the Korean innovative SMR to accident conditions. The work includes thermal-hydraulic system-code analysis, assessment of passive safety performance and comparison with component experiments.

Studies of containment cooling consider heat exchanger arrangements and heat removal paths. The aim is to connect component performance with the response of the reactor and containment.

Metal containment and flooding safety systems

금속격납용기 간극의 열전달 실험·CFD와 침수안전계통의 사고 완화 성능 평가

  • Metal containment vessel
  • Conjugate heat transfer
  • FSS

Metal containment studies examine heat transfer across the gap between structures under different conditions. CFD supports the analysis of normal-operation heat loss, while experiments address normal and accident conditions, including condensation and conjugate heat transfer.

The program also evaluates flooding safety concepts for natural-circulation SMRs. System-code transient studies inform design choices and the planning of a dedicated experimental facility. The containment heat transfer apparatus supports ongoing component research, while the dedicated FSS experiments remain in the planning stage.

Safety-related boiling and condensation experiments

표면 개질 응축, 풀비등·유동비등·임계열유속과 안전주입 열수력 실험

  • Condensation
  • Boiling and CHF
  • Fuel cladding

The heat transfer program studies condensation performance for passive containment cooling, including surface modification and depressurization. These experiments address the heat removal mechanisms relevant to containment integrity.

Related boiling studies cover pool boiling, flow boiling and critical heat flux (CHF). The program also investigates safety injection thermal hydraulics and compares fuel cladding surfaces, including Zircaloy-4 and chromium-coated specimens.

Code validation and uncertainty analysis

실험-해석 비교와 Wilks 방법·라틴 하이퍼큐브 샘플링을 활용한 불확실도 평가

  • Code validation
  • Wilks methodology
  • Latin hypercube sampling

Safety analysis includes comparison of code predictions with heat transfer experiments and assessment of uncertain model inputs. The uncertainty analysis workflow includes Wilks-based sample-size selection and Latin hypercube sampling (LHS).

We use these methods to examine the influence of input and model variations on thermal-hydraulic responses. The validation work links component-scale heat transfer behavior with the models used in system calculations.

PCM-based passive safety for the PMFR

상변화물질을 이용한 PMFR 피동 잔열 제거와 정상운전·사고조건 열성능 평가

  • PMFR
  • Phase change material
  • Decay heat removal

PMFR passive safety studies investigate phase change material (PCM) around the reactor vessel as a heat absorption mechanism. The research examines how the PCM response and heat transfer between structures contribute to decay heat removal.

The program considers this safety concept alongside primary-loop and helical-coil heat exchanger optimization. Helium-assisted fission product removal is another research topic relevant to fission product inventory and decay heat removal requirements.
