AI for Nuclear Safety

MAAP 기반 대리모델로 사고관리 조치에 따른 격납건물 압력 이력을 예측하고 전략을 비교

ATHENA develops AI methods to support the analysis of accident progression and management strategies. The September 2026 research presentation focuses on a neural-network surrogate trained on MAAP simulations. It maps an accident scenario and the timing of mitigation actions to a predicted containment pressure history, supporting comparison of candidate action schedules.

MAAP surrogate for severe accident management

사고 시나리오·조치 시작 시각을 입력받는 MAAP 신경망 대리모델과 GUI

  • MAAP
  • Neural network surrogate
  • SAMG

The model uses a database of MAAP severe accident simulations covering scenarios and SAMG action schedules. Inputs include the scenario and the start times of POSRV opening, reactor coolant system injection and containment spray.

The illustrated model predicts a 72-hour pressure history on a five-minute time grid. A GUI allows users to enter action timing and inspect the resulting history. The neural-network architecture is presented as a conceptual model.

Prediction of containment pressure history

조치 시각에 따른 격납건물 압력 변화와 설계압력 대비 여유 평가

  • Containment pressure
  • Action timing
  • Time-series prediction

The surrogate predicts the containment pressure response for a selected schedule of accident management actions. Predicted pressure histories allow comparison of the effect of changing action timing without rerunning every candidate directly in MAAP.

The illustrated example uses a total-loss-of-feedwater case and a selected action schedule. Its design-pressure reference and plotted values belong to that example and are not general acceptance criteria for other reactors.

Comparison of candidate accident management strategies

후보 조치 일정별 예측 압력과 안전여유 점수를 이용한 SAMG 의사결정 지원 연구

  • Strategy evaluation
  • Safety margin
  • Decision support

Candidate schedules are evaluated using the predicted pressure history and its margin relative to the design pressure of the modeled plant. The GUI presents the selected schedule alongside the distribution of scores for candidate schedules.

This work investigates a decision-support approach for SAMG strategy evaluation. The illustrative classifications summarize the model-based comparison for the example scenario.

Related AI research and future directions

기존 사고 진단·시계열 예측 연구를 바탕으로 사고관리 지원과 AI 신뢰성·규제 연구로 확장

  • Accident diagnosis
  • AI reliability
  • Research direction

Earlier ATHENA research includes accident diagnosis, prediction of plant states and source-term-oriented support tools. These studies provide related context for the current MAAP surrogate and strategy-comparison work.

The September 2026 research-history overview identifies AI-based accident prediction and regulation as a direction for the next research phase. Reliability assessment and the interpretation of AI predictions remain relevant topics as the support methods develop.
